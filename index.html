Programming Languages Lab

We build the theory and tools that let people reason about programs: what they compute, whether they are correct, and how to change them safely.

Department of Computer Science and Automation
Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru

Research

Our work sits where programming languages, formal methods and software engineering meet. We care about techniques that are sound and also work on real code.

⊑

Static analysis

Data-flow analysis, abstract interpretation and points-to analysis, including analyses for asynchronous message-passing systems and precise program slicing.

⊢

Program verification

Deductive verification and model checking of software, from operating-system kernels to controllers, and models and logics for real-time systems.

∃

Synthesis

Synthesising correct-by-construction controllers over infinite state spaces, and the meeting point of program synthesis and machine learning.

Joining the lab

Students join the lab through CSA's graduate programmes: M.Tech, M.Tech (Research) and PhD. Apply through IISc admissions, and mention your interest in programming languages and verification.

If you are already at IISc and want to work on a project or thesis with us, write to the faculty member whose work is closest to your interests. Project and postdoctoral openings, when available, are posted on the faculty home pages.
